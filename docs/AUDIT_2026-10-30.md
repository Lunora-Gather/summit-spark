## Remaining work

1. **P1: simultaneous cloud editing.** Conflict comparison occurs at sign-in;
   subsequent automatic `upsertRow` calls are last-write-wins. Another device can
   upload newer progress and have it overwritten by this device. Implement a
   server-validated version/transaction strategy, including stale-version,
   simultaneous write, first-row creation, logout and network-error tests with
   two independent authenticated sessions. A pre-upload GET alone would still
   leave a race. Until then, use one active device. Appwrite's
   [transaction conflict behavior](https://appwrite.io/docs/products/databases/tablesdb/transactions)
   is relevant, but must be validated with the pinned SDK and deployed service.
2. **P2: real device and complete route evidence.** Physical touch/controller
   feel, audio balance and uninterrupted human R1–R10 remain unverified. Keep
   authored geometry and physics stable until the playtest baseline exists.
3. **P3: gradual modularization.** The runtime still has roughly 12,700 lines and
   CSS roughly 8,300. Continue only behavior-preserving slices with direct
   consumers. Existing issues #2–#6 mix completed pure-data/helper extraction
   with pending orchestration work; do not close them based on filenames alone.

Gameplay rules, save schema, maps and UI layout are unchanged by this PR.
